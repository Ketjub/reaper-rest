WELCOME, TRAVELER

Spend the night at the Reaper's tavern.

Meet your friends in the castle courtyard. When the bell rings, walk into the tavern. The Reaper is behind the bar. Bet your gold, trust your luck, and try to pay the rent before the sun comes up.

BLACKJACK

Cards with the dead

Sit at the green table. The skeleton deals. Hit, stand, or lose your gold to the house.

RUSSIAN ROULETTE

Six chambers. One bullet.

Two players, one gun. The crowd bets on who walks away. Listen to the heartbeat… then pull.

THE COURTYARD

Mess around before the night

Fire your friends from a catapult. Lock them in the stocks. Throw tomatoes. Sit on the throne.

ONLINE WITH FRIENDS

Host. Share a code. Play.

One friend hosts, the others join with a code. Skeletons, witches, pumpkins and vampires welcome.

This game is still being made

The Reaper's Rest is a work in progress. All pictures, videos and text on this page come from an early build. The final game can look, sound and play different from what you see here. Features can change, move, or be removed.

THE DEVELOPER

Made by one indie dev in Bahrain

The Reaper's Rest is made by a solo indie game developer based in the Kingdom of Bahrain. The models, the code, the sounds and the music are all made in-house, one candle at a time.
